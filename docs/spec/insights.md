# Insights — Content Analysis

## Status: Placeholder — Out of MVP Scope

Insights is a **research extension** (Research Extension 1 in the proposal). It is explicitly out of MVP scope. No implementation work is planned for the MVP.

### Future direction (from `docs/ideas.md`)

An Insights mode accessed through a button that performs different analyses on the canvas and its contents/deltas:

- **Relevance analysis** — cells that significantly diverge from the topic or direction of the rest of the canvas
- **Staleness analysis** — cells not edited or interacted with for a significant amount of time
- **Correctness analysis** — cells containing information that does not align with what the PSC intended
